## Logo generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: final square 1024x1024 logo image for a Codex engineering plugin named Tact. Brand concept: deliberate judgment, precision, restraint, make the right change. Design a strong, original, extremely simple geometric uppercase T monogram. Flat warm vermilion-orange mark, on a perfectly uniform almost-black warm charcoal square background. The T consists of a broad solid horizontal cap and a solid centered vertical stem, joined with a precise narrow negative-space incision, with one restrained 45-degree cut at the cap's right end. A crisp memorable silhouette, optically centered, approximately 65 percent of canvas width and height, generous safe margins on all sides. Refined Swiss graphic design, flat vector-like edges, balanced negative space, strong contrast readable at 32px. No wordmark, no letters besides the abstract T, no tagline, no outline frame, no rounding of the square canvas, no border, no 3D, no mockup, no shadow, no gradients, no textures, no glow, no decorative circuitry, no sparkle, no watermark. Deliver exactly one finished icon asset.
