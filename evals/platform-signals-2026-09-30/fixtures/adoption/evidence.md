# Queue v2 migration records — synthetic fixture

Seventy of one hundred teams use Queue v2. A status slide calls the remaining thirty "resistant laggards" and proposes mandatory cutover plus a broad compatibility layer this quarter. The slide supplies no cost estimate or consumer analysis.

Six remaining teams use an offline signing appliance with the v1 callback format. Its firmware cannot parse v2 callbacks. One captured integration trace shows rejection of a v2 payload; the other five teams have the same appliance model but no captured replay. Queue v2 has no v1 callback adapter. Changing the appliance firmware requires vendor work and no vendor timeline is confirmed.

Eighteen remaining teams completed sandbox migration successfully. Their release managers recorded a month-end freeze and scheduled production moves in the next approved window. Three estimate half a day to update their deployment scripts. No failing v2 capability is reported by this cohort. Their schedules are proposals, not confirmed production completion.

Six teams have not answered the migration survey. Their usage and dependencies have not been inspected. Across all cohorts there are no current incident reports; v1 retirement has no approved date. The platform team can fund one bounded investigation this week, not a new migration program.
