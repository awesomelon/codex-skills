# Access records — synthetic fixture

The customer portal allows invited collaborators to download evidence bundles after their sponsor approves access. Invitation acceptance and sponsor approval are separate steps. Only approved users can create API tokens.

In a sample of twelve support cases from the last month, four external reviewers accepted invitations but saw a pending-access screen until the invitation expired after seven days. Two of those reviewers wrote that they abandoned the review and the customer postponed its sign-off. The other two outcomes are not recorded. No reviewer created a script or manual download process; none had token permission or a staff contact who could export on their behalf. Eight other cases concerned unrelated billing questions.

A product note says: "No one built a workaround, so access is probably not a major pain. Prioritize changing the pending-screen color instead." The support sample is not a random sample of all portal users. Global acceptance, approval, and abandonment telemetry is unavailable. Approval events for these four invitations have not been retrieved.

A separate cosmetic issue was reposted in five chat messages, all linking to the same original complaint. No active incident is in progress. Access policy still requires sponsor approval; no exception is authorized.
